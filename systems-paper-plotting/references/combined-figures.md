# Combined Figures

## Required Outputs

For multi-facet figures, produce:

- standalone figures for each facet;
- paper combined figures for the main text or appendix;
- complete overview figures containing every facet.

## Paper Combined Figures

Keep panel count modest, usually at most six panels per part. Split into multiple parts when necessary.

Use shared legends and compact facet titles. Share axes only when scales are comparable and readability is preserved.

## Complete Overview

The overview is for audit, not necessarily for publication. Enlarge the vector canvas or use multipage output rather than shrinking panels until unreadable.

## Consistency

Keep method order, colors, markers, hatches, line styles, component order, and normalization policy identical across standalone and combined outputs.

## Aggregation

Combined figures are not averages. Add aggregate panels only when the plan explicitly requests them and statistical meaning is sound.
