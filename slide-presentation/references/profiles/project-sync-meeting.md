# Profile: project sync meeting

A regular project meeting (weekly, monthly) where the team reports progress. The talk is results-oriented: what changed since last time, what it means, and what comes next.

## Audience

Collaborators and advisors who saw the previous sync. They remember the project's goal but not last time's numbers or the internal names.

## Planning

- Start from the progress since the last sync; that is the core of the talk.
- Open with a 1-2 slide recap of where the last sync ended (decisions, open questions).
- If the progress alone does not fill the slot, add the finished study most connected to it.
- Hold some finished material back for the next sync, so there is something to present even after a slow period. List what is held back in the spec's free section "Next time".
- Budget the time for the progress first, at about 100-110 words per minute.

## Structure

| Part | Role | Typical slides |
|---|---|---|
| Title | meeting name, date or period, authors | 1 |
| Recap | where the last sync ended: decisions and open questions | 1-2 |
| Progress | one slide per result: claim, evidence, why | most of the talk |
| Changes | what changed in plan, scope or assumptions, and why | 0-1 |
| Next | next steps and the questions for the group | 1 |
| Backup | detail no claim depends on | as needed |

## Content rules

- Results first: each progress slide states the finding as its title claim and shows the measurement that supports it, with where and how it was measured.
- Label each number as measured, model or estimate; state hardware, data and what is held fixed.
- Say what changed relative to last time when a number differs from a number shown before, so the audience does not suspect an error.
- Negative results and failed approaches count as progress; show them with the reason they failed.
- End with decisions or questions the group can answer in the meeting.

## Defaults

- Length: ask; syncs often run 10-30 minutes.
- Transitions still link slides, but a progress slide may hand over to the next result directly ("Next: ...").

## Extra checks

- [ ] The recap matches what the previous deck or notes actually said.
- [ ] Every progress slide has a measured or clearly labelled number.
- [ ] The "Next" slide lists concrete steps and open questions.
- [ ] Held-back material is listed in the spec for the next sync.
